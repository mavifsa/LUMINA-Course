Dalam tes TOEFL Structure, pertanyaan sering disamarkan dengan memperpanjang subjek memakai modifier kompleks atau memanipulasi participle dan clause untuk mengecoh peserta.

Finite vs Infinite Verb

Membedakan verb utama vs verb bayangan/modifier.

⚠️ Kesalahan Terbesar Peserta TOEFL (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall #1 Salah Menentukan Subject Utama

Mengira Noun Panjang / Prepositional Object sebagai Subject Berbeda.

❌ Salah: The key to the doors are missing.

✓ Benar: The key [to the doors] is missing.

Pitfall #2 Terkecoh Verb Tambahan

Mengira V-ing / V3 Berdiri Sendiri sebagai Verb Utama Kalimat.

❌ Salah: The man standing there is my uncle.

✓ Benar: The man [standing there] is my uncle.

Pitfall #3 Kebingungan Gerund & Infinitive

Bingung Membedakan Gerund/Infinitive sebagai Subjek vs Verb.

📌 Struktur: Reading books improves knowledge.

Pitfall #4 Terkecoh Noun Clause

Mengira Noun Clause Memiliki Dua Subjek yang Terpisah.

📌 Struktur: [What he said] shocked everyone.

Definisi Utama

Apa itu Subject?

Bagian kalimat yang melakukan tindakan, mengalami keadaan, atau menjadi topik utama dalam sebuah klausa.

5 Elemen yang Menjadi Subject dalam Kalimat:

1

Noun / Noun Phrase

Standar / Most Common
• Pekalongan is a city.
• Love builds a bridge.
• [The message sent by Almas] was deleted 5 minutes ago.
• [A number of students in the class] join an extra class for TOEFL.
2

Pronoun (Kata Ganti)

She, He, It, They, We, You, I
• She is intelligent.
• They arrived late.
• It seems difficult.
3

Gerund / Gerund Phrase (V-ing sebagai Noun)

Verb-ing as Singular Noun
• Reading improves vocabulary.
• Analyzing adjective clause part is hard.
• Listening, structures, and Reading are TOEFL sections. (Compound = Plural)
4

To-Infinitive / To-Infinitive Phrase

To + V1 as Singular Subject
• To understand syntax is difficult.
• To travel around the world is my biggest dream.
5

Noun Clause (Klausa Kata Benda)

Conj + S + V / Conj + V

Diawali kata hubung: what, that, whether, why, how, who.

• [What he said] shocked everyone.
• [Whether they agree] is uncertain.

Arsitektur Verb

Klasifikasi Verb dalam TOEFL Structure

Memahami kedudukan kata kerja dan fungsinya (Finite vs Infinite) adalah kunci utama menjawab soal TOEFL.

1. Berdasarkan Kedudukan (Ordinary vs Auxiliary)

Ordinary Verb

Kata Kerja Utama

Memberikan makna substantif dalam klausa. Pasti ada dalam setiap klausa utuh.

Auxiliary Verb

Kata Kerja Bantu

Membantu ordinary verb membentuk tenses, aspek, modalitas, atau penekanan.

Variasi Auxiliary Verb:

a. Primary Aux

be, do, have

b. Modal Aux

can, could, shall, should, may, must

Harus V1 bare setelah modal

c. Emphasize Aux

do / does / did (benar-benar)

2. Berdasarkan Fungsi (Finite vs Infinite Verb Matrix)

Bentuk Verb Status Finite Syarat & Penjelasan Aturan
V1 (Bare) FINITE Berdiri sendiri (Present) atau setelah Modal.
Vs / es, V2 ALWAYS FINITE Selalu berfungsi sebagai Kata Kerja Utama Kalimat.
V-ing CONDITIONAL Finite jika berpasangan dengan be (be + V-ing).
V3 (Past Participle) CONDITIONAL Finite jika berpasangan dengan be atau have.
To + Infinitive ALWAYS INFINITE Tidak pernah bisa menjadi Verb Utama Kalimat.
Laboratorium Analisis

Sentence Decomposition

Pelajari bagaimana kata kerja tampak (V-ing / V3) terbentuk akibat proses pemangkasan (reducing) klausa penjelas.

Case 1: Reduced Adjective Clause (V-ing) Active Reduction
"The students studying in the library are preparing for the TOEFL test."
🔍 Identifikasi Verb:

• studying = Infinite Verb

• are preparing = Finite Verb Utama

💡 Asal-Usul:

Asalnya: "The students [who are] studying..."

Case 2: Reduced Adverbial Clause (Having + V3) Perfective Participle
"Having completed the research, the professor submitted the article."
🔍 Identifikasi Verb:

• Having completed = Infinite Verb Phrase

• submitted = Finite Verb Utama (V2)

💡 Asal-Usul:

Asalnya: "[After the professor had] completed..."

Case 3: Reduced Passive Adjective Clause (V3) Passive Reduction
"The book written by the famous linguist has become popular."
🔍 Identifikasi Verb:

• written = Infinite Verb

• has become = Finite Verb Utama

💡 Asal-Usul:

Asalnya: "The book [which was] written..."
