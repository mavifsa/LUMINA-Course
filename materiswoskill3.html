Core Structure Axiom

Appositive : Informasi Tambahan

Appositive bersanding dengan noun pada berbagai posisi dan seringkali mengecoh peserta TOEFL dalam menentukan subjek serta verb utama kalimat.

01

Fungsi Noun Identitas

Menerangkan noun utama secara paralel.

02

Pola Punctuation

Diapit dua koma, satu koma, atau tanpa koma.

03

Variasi Bentuk Lengkap

Noun, Nouns, Gerund, To-Inf, Participle.

04

Menghindari Pengecohan

Tidak terkecoh mengira appositive sebagai subjek.

⚠️ Kesalahan Terbesar Peserta TOEFL

Pitfall #1 Subject-Verb Agreement Trap

Terkecoh Noun di dalam appositive

Contoh Sederhana:

❌ Salah: Qofa, a student from Tirto, have glasses.

✓ Benar: Qofa, [a student from Tirto], has glasses.

Contoh Tingkat Lanjut (Advance):

❌ Salah: Dr. Alfajri, along with his colleagues in the linguistics department, were conducting a seminar.

✓ Benar: Dr. Alfajri, [along with his colleagues...], was conducting a seminar.

Subjek utama (Dr. Alfajri) bersifat tunggal, sehingga verb wajib tunggal (was), terlepas dari noun apa pun yang ada di dalam appositive.

Pitfall #2 Missing Main Clause Verb Trap

Mengira appositive sebagai klausa utama.

Contoh Sederhana:

❌ Soal: The seminar, which was held in the main hall, _____ valuable insights.

✓ Jawaban: ...hall, provided valuable insights.

Contoh Tingkat Lanjut (Advance):

❌ Soal: The historical manuscripts, that were recently discovered in the archives and studied by experts, _____ significant linguistic data.

✓ Jawaban: ...experts, reveal significant linguistic data.

Bagian di antara koma adalah appositive (participle phrase). Kalimat tetap memerlukan Finite Verb utama seperti is untuk menyempurnakan struktur.

Blueprint & Variasi Bentuk

Apa itu Appositive?

Appositive adalah kata atau frasa benda yang menjelaskan noun lainnya. Berikut adalah 7 variasi bentuk appositive lengkap dengan analisis strukturnya:

7 Variasi Bentuk Appositive & Analisisnya:

1

Noun (Kata Benda Tunggal)

Single Noun
• We students must study hard. (Tanpa koma)
2

Noun Phrase (Frasa Kata Benda)

Noun Phrase
• Qofa, a student from Tirto, has glasses.
3

Gerund Phrase (Frasa V-ing)

Gerund Phrase
• My hobby, writing a journal article, has developed my skill.
4

To-Infinitive Phrase (Frasa To + V1)

To-Infinitive Phrase
• His dream, to get a high TOEFL score, comes true.
5

Proper Noun (Nama Diri)

Proper Noun
• My idol, Nurcholis Madjid, wrote many articles.
6

Participle Phrase (Reduced Relative Clause)

Participle Phrase
• Prof. Muhandis, leading the seminar on linguistics, answered the questions.
7

Leading Appositive (Appositive di Awal Kalimat)

Leading Appositive
• A renowned linguist and researcher, Dr. Alfajri published several books.
Positional Lab

Posisi Appositive dalam Kalimat

Appositive dapat mengikuti noun pada berbagai posisi: Subject, Object, maupun Object of Preposition.

1. Menerangkan Subject Subject Appositive
"Qofa, a student from Tirto, has a pair of glasses."
SubjectQofa
Appositivea student from Tirto
Verbhas
Objecta pair of glasses
2. Menerangkan Object Object Appositive
"I am interested to join BEC, the first English course in Pare."
Subject / VerbI am
ObjectBEC
Appositivethe first English course...
FunctionMenerangkan BEC
3. Menerangkan Obj of Prep Obj of Prep Appositive
"She spoke with Mr. Muhandis, the Head of the Language Development Unit."
Prepositional Phrase:

with Mr. Muhandis (with = prep, Mr. Muhandis = obj of prep).

Appositive:

the Head of the Language Development Unit menerangkan Mr. Muhandis.

Catatan:

Appositive dapat mengikuti noun di posisi objek preposisi.

Punctuation & Layout

Variasi Tanda Baca Appositive

Meskipun umumnya diapit oleh dua koma, appositive juga dapat muncul dengan satu koma atau bahkan tanpa koma sama sekali.

Diapit Dua Koma

Mid-Sentence Appositive

Appositive berada di tengah kalimat di antara noun yang diterangkan dan verb utama.

"Qofa, a student, studies hard."
Satu Koma (Akhir)

End-Sentence Appositive

Appositive diletakkan di akhir kalimat setelah noun utama.

"We visited Pare, the English village."
Tanpa Koma

Restrictive Appositive

Appositive menyatu erat tanpa koma karena memuat identitas spesifik.

"We students must study."
Uji Mandiri
