Asal Mula Adjective Clause: Dua Klausa Dijadikan Satu

Adjective clause lahir dari dua kalimat yang membicarakan noun yang sama. Kalimat kedua dipakai untuk menjelaskan noun tersebut. Noun yang berulang diganti conjunction (who, whom, which, that), lalu klausa itu diletakkan tepat di belakang noun yang dijelaskan.

Contoh Conjunction mewakili object
Klausa 1 (inti kalimat)
The man is Ferry Irwandi.
Klausa 2 (penjelas)
I met the man yesterday.
Digabung: adjective clause
The man whom I met yesterday is Ferry Irwandi.
Direduksi
The man whom I met yesterday is Ferry Irwandi.
The man I met yesterday is Ferry Irwandi.
  • Pada klausa 2, the man adalah object dari met (I met him).
  • The man diganti whom, lalu klausa dipindah ke belakang the man di klausa 1.
  • Setelah whom masih ada subject baru (I), jadi whom boleh dihapus saja. Makna tidak berubah.

Langkah Cepat: Lihat Apa yang Ada Tepat Setelah Conjunction

Conj + Subject + Verb → conj = object → hapus conjunction saja

Conj + Verb → conj = subject → hapus conjunction (+ be), verb berubah

The man whom I met is Ferry. → The man I met is Ferry.

The boy who speaks speaking at the front is Yusuf.

⚠️ Kesalahan Umum (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall #1 Menghapus Conjunction Subject Saja

Menghapus who/which/that yang berfungsi sebagai subject tanpa mengubah verb.

❌ The man saw me yesterday is Ferry.

✓ The man who saw me yesterday is Ferry.

Conjunction subject hanya boleh hilang bila verb berubah menjadi Ving / V3 / phrase.

Pitfall #2 Sisa Conjunction di Klausa Tereduksi

Menyisakan who/which sementara be sudah dibuang.

❌ Dollar which presented by him is oversimplified.

✓ Dollar presented by him is oversimplified.

✓ Dollar which is presented by him is oversimplified.

Pilih salah satu: reduksi penuh (tanpa conjunction) atau klausa utuh (conjunction + be).

Pitfall #3 Aktif vs Pasif Tertukar

Memakai Ving untuk makna pasif, atau V3 untuk makna aktif.

❌ The funds collecting by Ferry Irwandi ...

✓ The funds collected by Ferry Irwandi ...

❌ The teacher made us engage ...

✓ The teacher making us engage ...

Pitfall #4 Ving setelah Ordinal / Superlative / Only

Setelah the first, the smartest, the only, next, last, TOEFL memakai to infinitive.

❌ You are the only person understanding me.

✓ You are the only person to understand me.

Reduction Type 1

Penghapusan Conjunction Saja

Conjunction who, whom, which, that boleh dihapus hanya jika mewakili object dalam klausa tersebut.

Conjunction = Object Noun + [whom / which / that + Subject + Verb] → Noun + Subject + Verb

Setelah conjunction ada subject baru. Subject itu tetap menjadi pelaku verb, sehingga conjunction cukup dihapus dan makna tidak berubah.

Orang (whom)
The man whom I met is Ferry Irwandi.
Red ➭ The man I met is Ferry Irwandi.
whom / that
Every official whom / that the activists criticize is highly defensive.
Red ➭ Every official the activists criticize is highly defensive.
Benda (which / that)
The book that I read is Madilog.
Red ➭ The book I read is Madilog.
Conjunction = Subject Noun + [who / which / that + Verb] → tidak boleh hanya dihapus

Jika conjunction langsung diikuti verb, conjunction itu adalah subject. Menghapusnya begitu saja membuat kalimat kehilangan subject. Lihat Tab 3 untuk cara mereduksinya.

❌ The man who saw me yesterday is Ferry. → The man saw me yesterday is Ferry.
✓ The man who saw me yesterday is Ferry. / The man seeing me ... (sesuai Tab 3)
Reduction Type 2

Penghapusan Conj-Subject

Jika conjunction mewakili subject, hasil reduksi mengubah verb menjadi Participle (Ving / V3) atau menyisakan adjective / prepositional phrase / to infinitive, tergantung jenis kalimatnya.

a. Kalimat Aktif → Present Participle (Ving)

Buang conjunction, ubah verb menjadi Ving.

I like the teacher who makes making us engage in dialectics.
which/who + makes → making
The boy who speaks speaking at the front is Yusuf.
who + speaks → speaking

Untuk verb present / umum. Simple past aktif (who stole) tidak diubah menjadi Ving karena maknanya bergeser (lihat Skill 10).

b. Kalimat Pasif → Past Participle (V3)

Buang conjunction + be, sisakan V3.

The funds which were collected by Ferry Irwandi will be used to mitigate forest fires.
Dollar which is presented by him is oversimplified.
c. Menyisakan Adjective / Prepositional Phrase / To Infinitive Phrase

Menyisakan Adjective (who/which + be + adjective)

The man who is responsible for the MBG project is Sudaryono.

Menyisakan Prepositional Phrase (who/which + be + prep. phrase)

The committee that was in the contest blamed the participant's articulation.

Menjadi To Infinitive Phrase (setelah ordinal number, superlative, next, last, only)

Bentuk: to + V (aktif) atau to be + V3 (pasif). Setelah head ini, TOEFL memakai to infinitive, bukan Ving (the only person understanding).

Ordinal number (the first, second, next, last)
Bilal bin Rabah is the first man who performed to perform the adzan.
Superlative (the smartest, the best, the largest)
Nicolas Tesla is the smartest man who won to win the admiration of scientists worldwide.
The only
You are the only person who understands to understand me.
Makna pasif: to be + V3, atau ever + V3 (superlative + ever)
She was the second woman who was to be elected as rector.
It is the tallest building that has ever been ever built in the city.

Ringkasan Pola

Aktif   : conj + V → Ving

Pasif   : conj + be + V3 → V3

Be + adj / prep. phrase : conj + be dibuang → adj / prep. phrase

Ordinal / superlative / only / next / last : conj + V → to V (pasif: to be V3)

Verb utama : tetap mengikuti head noun → Every official criticized by the activists is ...

Restriction

🔑 Adjective Clause yang Tidak Boleh Direduksi

1. Non-Defining Clause (Appositive)

Jika klausa berada di antara tanda koma , ... , (informasi tambahan yang tidak esensial), koma dan konjungsinya tidak boleh dihilangkan secara sembarangan.

✓ My mother, who is cooking in the kitchen, is 50 years old.
❌ My mother, cooking in the kitchen, is 50 years old.

💡 Pola which is + noun phrase yang diubah menjadi appositive (mis. Jakarta, the capital of Indonesia, is ...) adalah bentuk lain yang dibahas di Skill 10. Pada soal, jangan menghapus conjunction non-defining tanpa alasan struktural yang jelas.

2. Conjunction Whose (Kepemilikan)

Whose tidak pernah bisa dihapus karena berfungsi menunjukkan kepemilikan.

✓ I know the man whose car was stolen. (Saya tahu pria yang mobilnya dicuri.)
❌ I know the man car was stolen.
❌ I know the man who car was stolen. (who bukan pengganti whose)

Ringkasan 3 Aturan

1. Conjunction = object → hapus conjunction saja.

2. Conjunction = subject → hapus conjunction, verb berubah (Ving / V3 / adj / prep. phrase / to V).

3. Non-defining (koma) dan whose → jangan direduksi sembarangan.
