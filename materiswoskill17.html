Core Structure Axiom

Setelah Than / As, Auxiliary Boleh Maju ke Depan Subject. Inversi Ini OPSIONAL.

Pada klausa pembanding, than + S + aux dan than + aux + S sama-sama benar. Bentuk inversi terdengar lebih formal. Hal yang diuji TOEFL adalah kecocokan auxiliary dengan verb utama, serta larangan inversi bila subject-nya pronoun.

Asal Mula: Dari Klausa Pembanding Utuh ke Inversi

Klausa setelah than sebenarnya klausa lengkap, tetapi verb yang sama dengan kalimat utama dihilangkan. Yang tersisa hanya subject dan auxiliary penggantinya.

Contoh Verb biasa: do-support
Klausa pembanding utuh
Hasan speaks Javanese better than Alfa speaks Javanese.
Normal: than + S + aux
Hasan speaks Javanese better than Alfa does.
Inversi: than + aux + S (opsional)
Hasan speaks Javanese better than does Alfa.
Subject pronoun: jangan inversi
✓ ... better than she does.  ❌ ... better than does she.
  • Does mewakili speaks Javanese yang tidak diulang.
  • Kedua bentuk bermakna sama. Bentuk inversi lebih formal.
  • Setelah does, verb utama tidak ditulis lagi.

Langkah Cepat Menyelesaikan Soal

1. Ada ekspresi perbandingan: more / -er ... than atau as ... as?

2. Lihat verb kalimat utama: be → be | verb biasa → do / does / did | perfect → have / has / had | modal → modal yang sama.

3. Tense mengikuti kalimat utama, agreement mengikuti subject di klausa pembanding.

4. Subject berupa pronoun → hanya bentuk normal (than she does).

5. Jika dua bentuk benar (normal dan inversi), keduanya diterima. Yang salah biasanya auxiliary-nya.

6. than / as + noun phrase saja (tanpa verb) berfungsi sebagai preposition: better than Alfa. Tidak ada aux dan tidak ada inversi.

⚠️ Kesalahan Umum (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall #1 Aux Tidak Cocok dengan Verb Utama

Kalimat utama memakai be, maka pembandingnya juga be, bukan do / does / did.

❌ The result was more accurate than did the second one.

✓ The result was more accurate than was the second one.

Pitfall #2 Inversi dengan Pronoun

Jika subject di belakang berupa pronoun, urutan tetap normal.

❌ Prof. Ilham knows more than does she.

✓ Prof. Ilham knows more than she does.

Pitfall #3 Agreement dengan Subject Belakang

Setelah inversi, aux harus sesuai dengan subject yang kini berada di belakangnya.

❌ ... more complex than was the previous schemes.

✓ ... more complex than were the previous schemes.

Pitfall #4 Verb Utama Diulang

Setelah aux pengganti, verb utama tidak ditulis lagi.

❌ Hasan speaks better than does Alfa speak.

✓ Hasan speaks better than does Alfa.

Fundamental Concept

Konsep dan Variasi Pola Inversi Perbandingan

Inversi dapat terjadi setelah ekspresi perbandingan yang memakai than atau as. Sifatnya opsional: bentuk normal dan bentuk inversi sama-sama benar.

Than / As: Preposition atau Conjunction?

Preposition

than / as + noun phrase

Tidak ada verb dan tidak ada auxiliary, sehingga tidak ada yang diinversi.

Hasan speaks Javanese better than Alfa.

Conjunction

than / as + klausa (S + aux)

Ada subject dan auxiliary pengganti, sehingga boleh diinversi.

Hasan speaks Javanese better than Alfa does.

Ketiga bentuk (than Alfa, than Alfa does, than does Alfa) bermakna sama. Inversi hanya mungkin bila than / as membuka sebuah klausa dan subject-nya noun, bukan pronoun. Pada pronoun, bentuk formal adalah than she does atau than she. Bentuk than her tergolong informal.

Variasi Pola

Setiap variasi menunjukkan bentuk normal dan bentuk inversinya.

A. Tanpa Auxiliary than / as + noun phrase
Hasan speaks Javanese better than Alfa.
The result of the first experiment was more accurate than the second one.
Naely was as curious about Comal city as her friends.

Perbandingan harus sepadan: noun dibandingkan dengan noun (hasil dengan hasil, orang dengan orang).

B. Comparative + Do-support than + S + do / does / did  ⇄  than + do / does / did + S
Normal
Hasan speaks Javanese better than Alfa does.
Inversi
Hasan speaks Javanese better than does Alfa.
Past
Hasan spoke Javanese better than did Alfa.
Subject plural
Students study harder than do teachers.
C. Comparative + Be than + S + be  ⇄  than + be + S
Normal
President Prabowo's flagship nutritional program is more logistically complex than the previous social assistance schemes were.
Inversi
President Prabowo's flagship nutritional program is more logistically complex than were the previous social assistance schemes.
Contoh lain
The result of the first experiment was more accurate than was the second one.
D. Comparative + Have (Perfect) than + S + have / has / had  ⇄  than + have / has / had + S
Normal
Alfa has studied longer than Hasan has.
Inversi
Alfa has studied longer than has Hasan.
E. Comparative + Modal than + S + modal  ⇄  than + modal + S
Normal
Rina can swim faster than her sister can.
Inversi
Rina can swim faster than can her sister.
Modal will
The new system will process the data faster than will the old one.
Modal could
Prof. Mawarida could explain the theory more clearly than could her assistant.
F. Equal: As ... As as + S + aux  ⇄  as + aux + S
Be, normal
Naely was as curious about Comal city as her friends were.
Be, inversi
Naely was as curious about Comal city as were her friends.
Do-support, inversi
Hasan speaks Javanese as fluently as does Alfa.

💡 Struktur ini sejajar dengan pola than dan bentuknya masih berterima, tetapi lebih jarang dan terdengar sangat formal. Soal TOEFL jauh lebih sering menguji pola dengan than.

G. Negative Equal: Not So / As ... As not so / as ... as + S + aux  ⇄  as + aux + S
Normal
Naely was not so curious about Comal city as her friends were.
Inversi
Naely was not so curious about Comal city as were her friends.
Ekspresi Perbandingan yang Berlaku

Comparative     : more / less / -er ... than

Equal           : as + adj / adv + as

Negative equal : not as / not so + adj / adv + as

Hal yang Perlu Diingat
  • Aux setelah than / as menggantikan verb yang sudah disebut di kalimat utama, sehingga verb itu tidak diulang.
  • Kedua bentuk bermakna sama. Bentuk normal lebih umum, bentuk inversi lebih formal dan sering dipilih ketika subject di belakang panjang.
  • Pada soal TOEFL, yang diuji biasanya auxiliary yang tepat, bukan memilih antara bentuk normal dan inversi.

Ringkasan Pola

Preposition : ... than / as + noun phrase  (tanpa aux)

Normal      : ... than / as + S + aux / be

Inversi     : ... than / as + aux / be + S

Syarat      : subject belakang = noun (bukan pronoun)

Auxiliary Selection

Memilih Auxiliary yang Tepat

Lihat verb pada kalimat utama, lalu pilih auxiliary pengganti yang sepadan.

a. Verb Biasa → do / does / did
Hasan speaks Javanese better than does Alfa. (present, subject tunggal)
Hasan spoke Javanese better than did Alfa. (past → did)
Students study harder than do teachers. (subject plural → do)
b. Be → is / am / are / was / were
The result of the first experiment was more accurate than was the second one.
The new program was more complex than were the previous schemes. (subject plural → were)
❌ ... was more accurate than did the second one. (be tidak digantikan do)
c. Perfect → have / has / had
Alfa has studied longer than has Hasan.
The students have studied longer than have the teachers.
d. Modal → modal yang sama
Rina can swim faster than can her sister.
The new system will process the data faster than will the old one.
Prof. Mawarida could explain the theory more clearly than could her assistant.
e. Dengan As ... As
Naely was as curious about Comal city as were her friends. (be → were, mengikuti 'her friends')
Hasan speaks Javanese as fluently as does Alfa.

Ringkasan Pemilihan Auxiliary

Kalimat utamaPengganti di klausa pembanding
verb biasa (V1 / V2)do / does / did
beis / am / are / was / were
have + V3have / has / had
modal + V1modal yang sama

Tense mengikuti kalimat utama, agreement mengikuti subject di klausa pembanding.

Restriction

🔑 Pembatasan dan Pola Pengecoh

1. Subject Belakang Berupa Pronoun

Seperti pada inversi tempat (Skill 14), inversi perbandingan tidak boleh dilakukan jika subject di bagian belakang berupa pronoun (he, she, it, they, we, you, I). Urutan tetap normal.

✓ Prof. Ilham knows more about linguistics than she does.
❌ Prof. Ilham knows more about linguistics than does she.

💡 Pada contoh ini she merujuk pada orang lain yang dibandingkan dengan Prof. Ilham.

2. Pola Pengecoh di TOEFL: Kesesuaian Auxiliary

Soal sering menguji apakah auxiliary pembanding sesuai dengan verb kalimat utama dan subject-nya.

The result of the first experiment was more accurate than _________.
(A) did the second one
(B) the second one did
(C) was the second one
(D) the second one being

Jawaban: (C). Verb kalimat utama adalah be (was), sehingga pembandingnya juga be, dan karena inversi, was berada di depan subject the second one.

(A) dan (B) salah karena memakai did. Do-support hanya untuk verb biasa, bukan be. (D) salah karena tidak ada verb finite.

Bentuk normal the second one was juga benar, tetapi soal TOEFL tidak akan menyajikan dua jawaban benar sekaligus, jadi yang dinilai adalah ketepatan auxiliary.

3. Tense dan Agreement
✓ Hasan spoke Javanese better than did Alfa. (past → did)
❌ Hasan spoke Javanese better than does Alfa. (tense tidak cocok)
✓ ... more complex than were the previous schemes.
❌ ... more complex than was the previous schemes. (subject plural)

Ringkasan Aturan

1. Inversi setelah than / as bersifat opsional.

2. Subject belakang pronoun → tanpa inversi.

3. Aux = sepadan dengan verb kalimat utama (be → be, verb biasa → do).

4. Verb utama tidak diulang setelah aux pengganti.

Kesimpulan Ringkas (Skill 16 & 17)
  • Inversi pengandaian: buang if, lalu pindahkan should, were, atau had ke depan subject. Type 2 tanpa were memakai pola Were + S + to V1.
  • Inversi perbandingan: opsional setelah than atau as. Majukan auxiliary / be ke depan subject. Tidak boleh bila subject belakang berupa pronoun (he, she, they, dan sebagainya).
