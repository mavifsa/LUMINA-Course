Core Structure Axiom

Correlative Conjunction: Pasangan Kata Hubung untuk Elemen Setara.

Terdiri dari 4 pasangan utama (both... and..., not only... but also..., either... or..., neither... nor...) yang wajib menghubungkan elemen gramatikal yang setara, serta mematuhi aturan kesesuaian verb (*proximity*) dan inversi klausa.

01

Ciri Utama

Selalu berpasangan & elemen setara.

02

Both... and...

Verb selalu berbentuk jamak (plural).

03

Proximity Rule

Verb mengikuti subjek terdekat.

04

Inversi Klausa

Wajib inversi jika diikuti klausa.

⚠️ Kesalahan Umum (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall #1 Parallelism Mismatch Trap

Menghubungkan dua elemen berbeda jenis (misal: noun dengan verb phrase) di antara pasangan correlative.

Contoh Jebakan:

❌ Salah: He wants both to study and working.

✓ Benar: He wants both to study and to work.

Elemen setelah kata pertama dan kata kedua pasangan konjungsi wajib setara gramatikalnya.

Pitfall #2 Inversion Failure on Not Only

Gagal melakukan inversi ketika frasa not only di awal kalimat diikuti oleh klausa utuh.

Contoh Jebakan:

❌ Salah: Not only he arrived late, but also...

✓ Benar: Not only did he arrive late, but also...

Ketika not only berada di awal kalimat, klausa pertamanya wajib mengalami inversi (auxiliary mendahului subjek).

Correlative Matrix

4 Anggota Utama Correlative Conjunction & Variasi Elemen

Pasangan kata hubung ini menghubungkan berbagai bentuk elemen setara (Noun, Verb, Adjective, Prepositional Phrase, hingga Clause).

1. Both __ and __ Keduanya ... dan ...
✓ Contoh Noun (Kata Benda):

"We need both laptop and mouse."

✓ Contoh Subject & Plural Verb:

"Both laptop and mouse are essential to work."

2. Not only __ but also __ Tidak hanya ... tapi juga ...
✓ Contoh Proximity Subject:

"Not only Tiara but also Tia likes Tio."

✓ Contoh Inversi Klausa:

"Not only did he arrive late, but also forgot documents."

3. Either __ or __ Baik ... atau ... (Salah satu)
✓ Contoh Prep. Phrase:

"The cat is either under the table or behind the sofa."

✓ Contoh Proximity Verb:

"Either the teachers or principal is responsible."

4. Neither __ nor __ Baik ... maupun ... tidak
✓ Contoh Object Setara:

"He washed neither his hands nor his face."

✓ Contoh Proximity Verb:

"Neither the manager nor the employees are willing."

Advanced Mechanics

Aturan Proximity (Subject-Verb Match) & Inversi Klausa

Dua aturan krusial dalam pengerjaan soal struktur TOEFL yang melibatkan correlative conjunction: penentuan bentuk verb dan pola inversi kalimat.

Aturan 1: Proximity Rule (Subject Terdekat) Either... or / Neither... nor / Not only... but also

Kecuali both... and... yang selalu menghasilkan verb plural (jamak), 3 jenis correlative lainnya menentukan bentuk verb (singular atau plural) berdasarkan subjek yang berada paling dekat dengan verb tersebut.

✓ Contoh Subjek Terdekat Singular:

"Not only Tiara but also Tia likes Tio."

Subjek terdekat 'Tia' (singular) ➔ verb menggunakan '-s' (likes).
✓ Contoh Subjek Terdekat Plural:

"Neither the manager nor the employees are willing."

Subjek terdekat 'employees' (plural) ➔ verb menggunakan 'are'.
Aturan 2: Inversi Klausa Not Only, Neither, Nor Inversion

Jika kata pertama dari correlative atau struktur negatif (seperti Not only atau Neither/Nor) diletakkan di awal kalimat dan diikuti oleh klausa utuh, maka klausa pertama tersebut wajib mengalami inversi (auxiliary verb mendahului subjek).

✓ Inversi Kalimat Verbal (Not Only):

"Not only did he arrive late, but also..."

Auxiliary 'did' mendahului subjek 'he'.
✓ Inversi Kalimat Nominal (Neither):

"Neither was the report accurate, nor..."

To be 'was' mendahului subjek 'the report'.
💡 Ringkasan Praktis TOEFL:
  • Both... and... ➔ Selalu Plural Verb.
  • Not only... but also / Either... or / Neither... nor ➔ Verb mengikuti subjek terdekat (proximity).
  • Not Only di awal kalimat ➔ Wajib Inversi pada klausa pertama.
