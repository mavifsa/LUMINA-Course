TOEFL Materials

Quick notes to help you understand the main TOEFL sections.

1. Listening Comprehension

Focus on the main idea, speaker intention, details, inference, and context clues. Listen for meaning rather than trying to translate every word.

  • Identify who is speaking and where the conversation takes place.
  • Notice paraphrases in the answer choices.
  • Pay attention to tone, contrast, and suggestions.

2. Structure & Written Expression

This section tests your ability to recognize grammatically correct sentences and identify errors.

  • Check subject–verb agreement.
  • Recognize noun, adjective, and adverb clauses.
  • Check parallel structure, verb forms, and modifiers.

3. Reading Comprehension

Read for the overall organization and locate evidence for each answer.

  • Skim the passage to identify its topic.
  • Scan for names, dates, and key terms.
  • Use context to infer unfamiliar vocabulary.

4. Suggested Practice Routine

Study one topic, complete a short exercise, review every explanation, and record recurring errors. Repeat the topic after a few days.
