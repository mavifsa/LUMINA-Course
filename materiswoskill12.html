Core Structure Axiom

AVC Reduction = Memadatkan Conj + S + V Menjadi Frasa, Asal Subject Kedua Klausa SAMA.

Adverbial clause (AVC) hanya boleh direduksi bila subject AVC = subject main clause. Setelah itu, tentukan: conjunction masih dipertahankan (Subject Reduction) atau ikut dibuang (Conj + Subj Reduction).

Asal Mula: Dari AVC Utuh Menjadi Frasa

AVC punya susunan conj + S + V. Ketika subject-nya sama dengan subject main clause, subject itu bersifat berulang sehingga boleh dibuang. Verb lalu menyesuaikan diri menjadi participle atau adjective.

Contoh 1 Subject Reduction (conj tetap)
AVC utuh
While the researchers were conducting the experiment, the researchers recorded every observation.
Syarat
Subject AVC = subject main clause (the researchers). Boleh direduksi.
Direduksi
While the researchers were conducting the experiment, the researchers recorded every observation.
While conducting the experiment, the researchers recorded every observation.

Subject + be dibuang, while tetap ada, verb tersisa Ving.

Contoh 2 Conj + Subj Reduction (conj ikut hilang)
AVC utuh
Because the researchers lacked sufficient funding, the researchers postponed the project.
Syarat
Subject sama. Because (Reason) tidak boleh bertemu Ving, jadi conj wajib hilang.
Direduksi
Because the researchers lacked sufficient funding, the researchers postponed the project.
Lacking sufficient funding, the researchers postponed the project.

Conj + subject dibuang, lacked menjadi Lacking.

Langkah Cepat Mereduksi AVC

1. Cek subject: AVC = main clause? Jika tidak → jangan direduksi.

2. Jenis AVC: Reason (because, since, as) → Conj + Subj. Contrast → conj tetap. Time / Condition → conj tetap atau hilang.

3. Bentuk verb: aktif → Ving | pasif → V3 | be + adj → adj | sudah selesai lebih dulu → having V3

4. Posisi: Subject Reduction → I / M / F. Conj + Subj → I / M.

⚠️ Kesalahan Umum (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall #1 Dangling Modifier

Subject main clause bukan pelaku frasa reduksi.

❌ Walking to campus, a wallet was found by Usamah.

✓ Walking to campus, Usamah found a wallet.

Yang berjalan adalah Usamah, bukan dompet. Subject setelah koma harus pelaku frasa.

Pitfall #2 Because + Ving / Adj

Conj Reason (because, since, as) tidak boleh tersisa di depan hasil reduksi.

❌ Because lacking sufficient funding, the researchers postponed ...

✓ Lacking sufficient funding, the researchers postponed ...

✓ Because they lacked sufficient funding, ... (klausa utuh)

Pitfall #3 Aktif vs Pasif Tertukar

Ving untuk makna aktif, V3 untuk makna pasif.

❌ When submitting on time, the reports will be processed.

✓ When submitted on time, the reports will be processed.

❌ Built the report, the team presented it.

✓ Having built the report, the team presented it.

Pitfall #4 Membuang Conj yang Menjaga Makna

Conj contrast (although, though) dan before sebaiknya tetap ada agar makna tidak berubah.

✗ Tired, he continued working. (pertentangan hilang)

✓ Although tired, he continued working.

✓ Before leaving, she locked the door.

Fundamental Rules

Syarat, Bentuk Hasil, dan Posisi

Hanya 4 jenis AVC yang bisa direduksi: Time, Contrast, Reason, Condition.

Syarat Mutlak Subject AVC = Subject Main Clause

Frasa hasil reduksi tidak punya subject sendiri, sehingga ia meminjam subject main clause. Jika subject berbeda, hasilnya dangling modifier (salah secara grammar).

✓ While Usamah was walking to campus, Usamah found a wallet. → While walking to campus, Usamah found a wallet.
❌ While Usamah was walking to campus, a wallet was lost. → While walking to campus, a wallet was lost.
4 Bentuk Hasil Reduksi
BentukDipakai untukContoh

V3Makna pasifWhen submitted on time, ...
Adj / Adj Phrasebe + adjective (juga be + prepositional phrase)Although tired from the long flight, ...
Having V3 / Having been V3Kejadian di AVC selesai lebih dulu sebelum main clause (aktif / pasif)Having completed the course, ...
Posisi Hasil Reduksi Initial (I) / Middle (M) / Final (F)
InitialInitial: frasa di depan, diikuti koma.
While walking to campus, Usamah found a wallet.
Middle: frasa disisipkan setelah subject, diapit dua koma.
Usamah, while walking to campus, found a wallet.
Final: frasa di akhir kalimat, umumnya tanpa koma.
Usamah found a wallet while walking to campus.

Aturan praktis:

  • Subject Reduction (conj tetap): bisa di Initial, Middle, dan Final.
  • Conj + Subj Reduction (conj hilang): umumnya hanya Initial dan Middle. Posisi Final dihindari karena tanpa conj hubungan logisnya kabur dan frasa bisa dikira menerangkan noun terdekat.
  • AVC of Reason hampir selalu muncul sebagai Conj + Subj Reduction sehingga biasanya di Initial atau Middle, bukan Final.

Reduction Type 1

Subject Reduction (Conjunction Tetap)

Subject (dan be) dibuang, conjunction tetap tampil. Berlaku untuk AVC of Time, Contrast, dan Condition. AVC of Reason tidak masuk kategori ini.

Conjunction yang berlaku

Time      : when, while, after, before, since, once

Contrast : although, though, while

Condition: if, unless

Reason    : tidak bisa (because + Ving / adj tidak berterima)

a. Aktif → Conj + Ving
Asli
While the researchers were conducting the experiment, they recorded every observation carefully.
IWhile conducting the experiment, the researchers recorded every observation carefully.
MThe researchers, while conducting the experiment, recorded every observation carefully.
FThe researchers recorded every observation carefully while conducting the experiment.

Subject the researchers + were dibuang. Maknanya aktif ("sedang melakukan") sehingga tersisa While + Ving.

b. Pasif → Conj + V3
Asli
When the reports are submitted on time, they will be processed immediately.
IWhen submitted on time, the reports will be processed immediately.
MThe reports, when submitted on time, will be processed immediately.
FThe reports will be processed immediately when submitted on time.

Subject AVC (the reports) + are dibuang. Klausa asli pasif sehingga tersisa When + V3. Perhatikan: pronoun they di main clause berganti menjadi noun the reports pada posisi Initial, karena subject AVC sudah tidak ada untuk dirujuk.

💡 Conj if, unless, once (dan when bermakna "jika") paling sering diikuti V3 atau adjective: If asked, ... / Unless otherwise stated, ... / Once completed, ...
c. Be + Adjective / Prepositional Phrase → Conj + Adj / Prep. Phrase
Asli
Although he was tired from the long flight, he continued working.
IAlthough tired from the long flight, he continued working.
MHe, although tired from the long flight, continued working.
FHe continued working although tired from the long flight.

Pola asli subject + be + adjective. Setelah subject dan be dibuang, conjunction langsung bertemu adjective (tired).

Dengan prepositional phrase
While he was in Jakarta, he visited the old city. → While in Jakarta, he visited the old city.
d. Being / Been pada Pasif

Pada pasif, being boleh dipertahankan atau dihilangkan. Keduanya benar.

While (being) questioned by the police, the suspect remained calm.

Setelah subject dibuang, conjunction (while, after, when) berfungsi seperti preposition, sehingga bentuk Ving di belakangnya bersifat gerund: after marrying you, while singing.

Ringkasan Pola

Aktif   : conj + S + V → conj + Ving

Pasif   : conj + S + be + V3 → conj + (being) V3

Be + adj / prep. phrase : conj + S + be + adj → conj + adj

Posisi  : IMF semua boleh

Reduction Type 2

Conj + Subj Reduction (Conjunction Ikut Dibuang)

Conjunction dan subject sama-sama dibuang. Kalimat langsung dibuka oleh participle atau adjective. Hubungan makna kini ditangkap dari konteks.

Conjunction yang berlaku

Time      : after, while, when  (before tidak: conj harus tetap, "Before leaving, ...")

Condition: if

Reason    : because, since, as  (WAJIB menggunakan jenis ini)

Contrast  : tidak dipakai (conj hilang = pertentangan hilang)

Posisi: dominan I dan M. Posisi F dihindari karena tanpa conjunction hubungan logis kalimat menjadi kabur.
a. Aktif → Ving
Asli
Because the researchers lacked sufficient funding, they postponed the project.
ILacking sufficient funding, the researchers postponed the project.
MThe researchers, lacking sufficient funding, postponed the project.

Because dan the researchers dibuang, verb aktif lacked menjadi Lacking.

Time (while / when)
While Usamah was walking to campus, he found a wallet. → Walking to campus, Usamah found a wallet.
b. Pasif → V3
Asli
Since the laboratory was built with outdated equipment, it could not conduct the experiment.
IBuilt with outdated equipment, the laboratory could not conduct the experiment.
MThe laboratory, built with outdated equipment, could not conduct the experiment.

Klausa asli pasif (was built). Conj, subject, dan was dibuang sehingga langsung V3. Bentuk Being built juga benar.

c. Be + Adjective → Adjective
Asli
As the professor was exhausted, he canceled the lecture.
IExhausted, the professor canceled the lecture. (atau: Being exhausted, ...)
MThe professor, exhausted, canceled the lecture.

Being sering redundan karena adjective sudah cukup menjelaskan kondisi subject.

d. Perfect Participle → Having V3 / Having been V3
Asli (Time: after)
After the PIBI students had completed the TOEFL preparation, they took the real test.
IHaving completed the TOEFL preparation, the PIBI students took the real test.
MThe PIBI students, having completed the TOEFL preparation, took the real test.

Dipakai bila kegiatan di AVC selesai lebih dulu sebelum main clause (makna "setelah"), dan juga lazim pada Reason. Aktif: Having + V3. Pasif: Having been + V3.

Pasif
After the volunteers had been trained by experts, they were ready to help. → Having been trained by experts, the volunteers were ready to help.

Ringkasan Pola

Aktif      : conj + S + V → Ving

Pasif      : conj + S + be + V3 → V3 (atau Being V3)

Be + adj   : conj + S + be + adj → adj (atau Being adj)

Selesai dulu : conj + S + had V3 → Having V3 / Having been V3

Posisi      : IM (hindari F)

Kesimpulan
  • AVC Reduction memadatkan kalimat akademik tanpa mengubah makna, dengan syarat subject kedua klausa sama.
  • Subject Reduction: conjunction masih terlihat (While, When, Although, If).
  • Conj + Subj Reduction: conjunction hilang, kalimat dibuka langsung oleh Ving / V3 / adjective.
  • Reason (because, since, as) tidak boleh bertahan di depan hasil reduksi, sehingga wajib memakai Conj + Subj Reduction.
