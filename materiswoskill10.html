Core Structure Axiom

Adjective Clause (AC) Berfungsi Menerangkan Head (Noun / Pronoun).

AC bertindak sebagai kata sifat dalam bentuk klausa yang diletakkan langsung setelah noun, indefinite pronoun, demonstrative pronoun, atau personal pronoun yang diterangkannya.

⚠️ Kesalahan Umum (Common Pitfalls)

Pitfall #1 Redundant Pronoun Trap

Menulis ulang pronoun objek (him, it, them) setelah relative pronoun.

❌ The man whom I met him is Ferry.

✓ The man whom I met is Ferry.

Relative pronoun sudah menggantikan noun tersebut, jadi pronoun tambahan tidak diperlukan.

Pitfall #2 Who vs. Whom

Memakai whom saat klausa membutuhkan subjek, atau sebaliknya.

❌ The boy whom helped her ...

✓ The boy who helped her ...

Setelah relative pronoun ada verb → who (subject). Setelah relative pronoun ada subjek baru → whom (object).

Pitfall #3 Subject-Verb Agreement di dalam AC

Pada RPS, verb mengikuti head noun, bukan noun terdekat.

❌ The books that is on the shelf are mine.

✓ The books that are on the shelf are mine.

Pada RPO, verb justru mengikuti subjek baru: "The books that she writes ..."

Pitfall #4 Reduksi yang Salah Bentuk

Memakai V-ing / V3 setelah ordinal number, superlative, atau the only; atau meninggalkan sisa "who/which" di klausa tereduksi.

❌ She is the first woman winning the prize.

✓ She is the first woman to win the prize.

❌ Students who studying here ...

✓ Students studying here ... / who are studying here ...

Relative Pronouns

RPS (Subject) dan RPO (Object)

Perbedaan utamanya: apa yang ada tepat setelah relative pronoun. Jika verb, itu RPS. Jika subjek baru, itu RPO.

RPS: Relative Pronoun as Subject Noun + [who / which / that + Verb ...]

Relative pronoun langsung diikuti verb. RP tidak boleh dihilangkan. Verb mengikuti head noun. Pilihan RP: who / that (orang), which / that (benda).

Noun + who + V
The man who speaks is Ferry Irwandi.
Noun + which + V
I like Gus Ulil's delivery which makes us engage.
Noun + that + modal + V
Yusuf is a boy that can speak Arabic.
Noun + who + be + V-ing
The students who are waiting outside are nervous.
Noun + which + be + V3 (pasif)
The manuscript which was reviewed by Nur Hayati is accepted.
Plural head → verb plural
The books that are on the shelf belong to Sinta.
Indefinite / demonstrative + who + V
Anyone who cheats will be disqualified. / Those who arrive late must wait.
RPO: Relative Pronoun as Object Noun + [whom / which / that / ∅ + Subject + Verb ...]

Relative pronoun diikuti subjek baru dan verb. Verb mengikuti subjek baru itu, bukan head noun. Pilihan RP: whom / that (orang), which / that (benda), atau dihilangkan (zero pronoun).

Noun + whom + S + V
The man whom I met is Ferry Irwandi.
Noun + that + S + V
The book that I read is Madilog.
Noun + which + S + V
The software which we installed is free.
Noun + ∅ + S + V (RP dihilangkan)
I have something you like. / The book I read is Madilog.
Subjek baru lebih panjang
The theory which the author explained in chapter two is controversial.
Verb mengikuti subjek baru
The books that she writes are popular.

Perbedaan Struktur dalam Satu Lihat

RPS : The man who saw me ... (RP + verb)

RPO : The man whom I saw ... (RP + subjek + verb)

RPS tidak bisa dihilangkan; RPO bisa (The man I saw).

Relative Adverbs & Possessive

Relative Adverbs (Where, When, Why) & Whose

Relative Adverbs

Setelah where / when / why, klausa harus lengkap (S + V). Konstruksi: Noun + where / when / why + S + V

WHERE (= in / at / on which) bisa dihubungkan dengan:

place, city, country, house, building, area, region, situation

Demak is the city where Raden Patah established the sultanate.

WHEN (= in / on / during which) bisa dihubungkan dengan:

time, day, year, moment, period, season, century, era

I remember the moment when Gus Dur was impeached.

WHY (= for which) bisa dihubungkan dengan:

reason

Many people know the reason why the movie was blocked.

Where atau which?

The city where I lived is Kudus. ✓ (klausa lengkap)
The city which I visited is Kudus. ✓ (visited butuh objek)
❌ The city where I visited last week ...
Possessive: Whose

Menyatakan kepemilikan (his/her/its/their). Konstruksi: Noun + whose + Noun (tanpa article) + V

I met the writer whose book is very famous.
The researcher whose book I reviewed is a professor. (whose + noun + S + V)
The building whose roof collapsed was old. (head benda)
❌ whose the book / who's book / whose his book
Preposition + Whom / Which

Preposisi boleh di depan RP (formal, hanya whom/which) atau tetap di akhir klausa.

The professor to whom I spoke is from Leiden.
The professor (whom) I spoke to is from Leiden.
❌ The professor to that I spoke ... / The professor whom I spoke ...
Adjective Clause Reduction

Reduksi Adjective Clause

Reduksi dengan V-ing, V3, dan phrase hanya untuk RPS. Pada RPO, konjungsi (RP) cukup dihilangkan.

1. Menjadi V-ing (aktif)

Buang RP + be, atau buang RP lalu ubah verb (present) menjadi V-ing.

The man who is speaking is my professor.
Participants who are not attending the session will be contacted.
A policy which affecting millions of workers was approved. (which affects → affecting)
Anyone who wishing to join must register. (who wishes → wishing)
2. Menjadi V3 / Past Participle (pasif)

Buang RP + be, sisakan V3.

The manuscript which was reviewed by Nur Hayati is accepted.
The bridge which is being built will open soon.
The candidates who have been selected will be notified.
3. Menjadi Prepositional / Adjective Phrase / Appositive

Jika predikat berupa be + PP / adjective / noun, buang RP + be. Noun phrase menjadi appositive dengan koma.

be + PP
The book which is on the table is mine.
be + adjective phrase
The students who are eager to learn improved quickly.
be + single adjective (pindah ke depan noun)
The people who are diligent succeed. → The diligent people succeed.
be + noun (appositive)
Jakarta, which is the capital of Indonesia, is a megacity.
4. Menjadi With / Having

Verb have/has diganti with (paling natural) atau having.

A house which has with three bedrooms is for sale.
Students who have having completed the course may take the exam.
5. Menjadi To Infinitive

Dipakai setelah head tertentu. Bentuk: to + V (aktif), to be + V3 (pasif), not to + V (negatif). RP dan modal dibuang. Setelah head ini, V-ing salah (the first man walking).

Ordinal number (the first, second, third, next, last)

Neil Armstrong was the first man who walked to walk on the moon.
She was the second woman who was to be elected as rector.
The next train that will to arrive at platform 2 is express.

Superlative (the best, the most important, the youngest, the largest)

She is the youngest student who has to win the olympiad.
This is the best way that we can to learn vocabulary.
It is the tallest building that has ever been ever built in the city. (pasif + ever → ever V3)

The only (the only person / one / way / thing)

Rina was the only candidate who passed to pass all the tests.
The only thing that can be to be done is to wait.
She was the only one who did not to complain.

Indefinite pronoun (something, anything, nothing, someone, anyone, no one)

I need someone who can to help me.
She has nothing that she can to wear.
He has no one whom he can to talk to. (preposisi tetap ada)
She wants something interesting to read. (adjective sebelum to V)

Indefinite quantity (much, many, little, few, a lot of, plenty of, enough, several)

There is a lot of work that we have to do.
She has little time that she can to spare.
There are many problems that must be to be solved.
We have enough money to buy a laptop.

💡 Bedakan makna: someone to help me = yang dapat membantu; someone helping me = yang sedang membantu.

Yang Tidak Bisa Direduksi
RPO (ada subjek baru) → hanya RP dihilangkan
The man met is Ferry. → The man whom I met is Ferry.
Simple past aktif → makna bergeser
The man stealing the bag was arrested. → The man who stole the bag was arrested.
Whose
The writer book is famous. → The writer whose book is famous.
Preposition + whom/which
The method with analyzing ... → The method with which we analyzed ...
Where / when / why
the reason why he left → the reason he left
Modal di luar special head
A tool can detecting plagiarism → A tool which can detect plagiarism
